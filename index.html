About us

Cargo and bilty tracking in Pakistan

Kashaf Lasani Cargo Services (KLC) is a cargo and transport company in Pakistan. Our main route is Sialkot to Karachi, and we also serve Gujranwala and Lahore.

Customers can track a bilty online with the bilty number, book a bilty for cartons, bales and part loads, and see port status on the News & Updates page. Local pickup in Sialkot and port delivery in Karachi can be added to a booking. Business clients get an online ledger. Export cargo is delivered to KPT, QFS, Port Qasim and the dry ports.

To book or ask a question, call or WhatsApp 0300-6166561 or 0307-6401597, call the office on 052-4587207, or email kashaf.lasani@gmail.com.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is KLC?

Kashaf Lasani Cargo Services (KLC) is a cargo and transport company in Pakistan. It runs Sialkot to Karachi cargo, takes bilty bookings, and lets customers track a bilty online.

How do I track my bilty?

Type your bilty number on the Home page, for example 151090, and press Track. You see the status, the route, the status dates and, once the goods have arrived, who to contact at the destination.

What does tracking show, and what does it not show?

It shows the bilty number, status, where the goods are, the route and the contact person at the destination. To protect your privacy it never shows sender or customer names, quantity, weight or charges. After delivery it shows only the name of the person who received the goods.

Can I track with my mobile number?

No. Tracking works with the bilty number only, which is written on your bilty slip. If you cannot find it, send us a message on WhatsApp at 0300-6166561.

Can I get my bilty as a PDF?

The website is for tracking only. To get a PDF copy of your bilty, ask the KLC team on WhatsApp at 0300-6166561.

How do I book a bilty?

Choose Book a Bilty and fill the short form. Your booking is written out and ready to send to KLC by WhatsApp or email. KLC confirms it, makes the final bilty on SmartCargo and sends you the bilty number.

What are the cargo rates?

Charges depend on the route, the weight or CBM, local pickup and port delivery. KLC confirms the charges when it confirms your booking.

Which cities do you serve?

KLC serves Sialkot, Gujranwala, Lahore and Karachi. KLC's own main route is Sialkot to Karachi.

Does KLC send cargo from Sialkot to Karachi?

Yes. Sialkot to Karachi is the main KLC route. You can book a bilty on this website and track it until delivery.

How can I contact Kashaf Lasani Cargo Services?

Call or WhatsApp 0300-6166561 or 0307-6401597, call the office on 052-4587207, or email kashaf.lasani@gmail.com.

Is cargo charged by weight or by volume?

A bilty can be booked with the weight in kg, the volume in CBM, or both. KLC confirms the charges when it confirms your booking.

Does KLC deliver export cargo to the port?

Yes. KLC delivers export cargo to KPT, QFS, Port Qasim and the dry ports. Port status and vessel dates are on the News & Updates page.

Can business clients see a ledger online?

Yes. Business clients with a KLC account code can open My Account to see their bilties, payments and balance.
